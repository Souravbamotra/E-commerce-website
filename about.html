📖 Our Story

It started in a college hostel in Pune, 2022. Three passionate otakus — Arjun, Priya, and Rishi — were frustrated with waiting 6 weeks for international shipping, paying absurd customs duties, and receiving fake products that looked nothing like the pictures.

They had a simple idea: create a trusted, premium anime merchandise destination for India. Source directly from authentic manufacturers, bring it into India legally, quality-check every single piece, and ship it with care.

Today, OtakuVault is trusted by 10,000+ anime fans across 500+ Indian cities. We stock 200+ products across 12+ franchises — from Naruto to JJK, One Piece to Demon Slayer. Every product is authentic, quality-checked, and shipped with love.

This is just the beginning. Welcome to the Vault. 🔥
